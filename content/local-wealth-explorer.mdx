## Overview

Local Wealth Explorer helps users navigate wealth indicators across neighborhoods and census tracts with a map-first workflow designed for practical analysis.

## Technical Breakdown

This project combines **BigQuery** for data querying, **Next.js** and **React** for the application layer, and a geospatial visualization stack including **D3**, **Mapbox**, **deck.gl**, and **Carto for React**.
